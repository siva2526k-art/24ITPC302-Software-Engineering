# 24ITPC302: Textbook Catalog & Chapter Concordance

This catalog documents the official prescribed textbooks and reference literature for **24ITPC302 – Software Engineering**.

---

## 📚 Prescribed Textbooks (Syllabus Prescribed)

### 1. Roger S. Pressman
- **Title:** *Software Engineering: A Practitioner's Approach*
- **Edition:** 6th / 7th / 8th Edition, McGraw-Hill International Edition.
- **Key Coverage:**
  - Prescriptive Models (Waterfall, V-Model, Spiral, RAD): Chapters 2 & 3
  - Agile & Extreme Programming: Chapters 4 & 5
  - Requirements Engineering & SRS: Chapters 5, 6, 7
  - Software Design Concepts, Cohesion, Coupling, Architecture: Chapters 9, 10, 11, 12
  - Software Testing (Basis Path, Black-box, Integration): Chapters 17, 18, 19
  - Project Management, Metrics (FP, COCOMO), Scheduling: Chapters 24, 25, 26, 27
  - Risk Management & CASE Tools: Chapters 28 & 31

### 2. Ian Sommerville
- **Title:** *Software Engineering*
- **Edition:** 7th / 8th / 9th Edition, Pearson Education.
- **Key Coverage:**
  - Socio-technical systems, Software processes: Chapters 2 & 3
  - Requirements engineering: Chapter 4
  - System modeling & Architectural design: Chapters 5 & 6
  - Software testing & Quality management: Chapters 8, 9, 24
  - Project management & Planning: Chapters 22 & 23

### 3. Gene Kim, Jez Humble, Patrick Debois, John Willis
- **Title:** *The DevOps Handbook: How to Create World-Class Agility, Reliability, & Security in Technology Organizations*
- **Edition:** 2nd Edition, 2016, IT Revolution Press.
- **Key Coverage:**
  - Modern Agile deployment, Continuous Integration / Continuous Delivery (CI/CD)
  - Telemetry, feedback loops, and organizational learning.

## 📖 Reference Literature
1. **Rajib Mall**, *Fundamentals of Software Engineering*, 3rd / 4th Edition, PHI Learning.

3. **S. A. Kelkar**, *Software Engineering*, Prentice Hall of India Pvt Ltd, 2007.
4. **Stephen R. Schach**, *Software Engineering*, Tata McGraw-Hill, 2007.
