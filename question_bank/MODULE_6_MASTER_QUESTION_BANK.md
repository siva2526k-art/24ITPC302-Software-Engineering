## PART A: 2-MARK CONCEPTUAL QUESTIONS (K1 / K2)

1. **Question 6.1:** What is the difference between a Reactive and a Proactive Risk Strategy?  
   *(Answer: Reactive ("firefighting") waits until a risk materializes and causes a crisis; Proactive formally anticipates, plans mitigation actions, and continuously monitors risk triggers before they occur).*
2. **Question 6.2:** Define Risk Exposure ($RE$). What is its formula?  
   *(Answer: $RE = P \times C$, where $P$ is the estimated probability of risk occurrence ($0 \le P \le 1$) and $C$ is the cost / impact of the risk if it occurs).*
3. **Question 6.3:** Differentiate between Known Risks, Predictable Risks, and Unpredictable Risks.  
   *(Answer: Known risks are uncovered after careful project review [e.g. tight deadlines]; Predictable risks are extrapolated from past project history [e.g. staff attrition]; Unpredictable risks are true unknown black swans [e.g. vendor bankruptcy]).*
4. **Question 6.4:** What does the acronym RMMM stand for in software risk management?  
   *(Answer: Risk Mitigation, Monitoring, and Management).*
5. **Question 6.5:** Distinguish between Risk Mitigation and Risk Management.  
   *(Answer: Risk Mitigation consists of proactive steps taken *before* the risk occurs to prevent it; Risk Management consists of contingency plans executed *after* the risk occurs to contain the damage).*
6. **Question 6.6:** What is the purpose of an Object Management System (OMS) in a CASE environment?  
   *(Answer: Acts as the central project database/repository storing all software artifacts [specifications, designs, code, test suites] and maintaining their interrelationships, dependencies, and version histories).*
7. **Question 6.7:** Distinguish between Upper CASE and Lower CASE tools.  
   *(Answer: Upper CASE tools assist early development phases [Requirements elicitation, planning, DFD/UML design modeling]; Lower CASE tools assist late implementation phases [compilers, debuggers, test automation, static analyzers]).*
8. **Question 6.8:** What is an Integrated CASE (I-CASE) environment?  
   *(Answer: A unified software engineering suite that seamlessly connects upper and lower CASE tools across a common central repository, providing end-to-end traceability from SRS to deployment).*
9. **Question 6.9:** What is Risk Reduction Leverage ($RRL$)? How is it interpreted?  
   *(Answer: $RRL = \frac{RE_{\text{before}} - RE_{\text{after}}}{\text{Cost of Mitigation Action}}$. If $RRL > 1.0$, the proposed risk mitigation investment is cost-effective).*
10. **Question 6.10:** State two tangible and two intangible benefits of deploying CASE tools in an enterprise.  
    *(Answer: Tangible: Automated code generation, automated consistency checks; Intangible: Higher architectural quality, improved long-term maintainability).*
