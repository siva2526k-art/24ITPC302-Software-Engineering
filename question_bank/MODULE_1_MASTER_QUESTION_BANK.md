## PART A: 2-MARK CONCEPTUAL QUESTIONS (K1 / K2)

1. **Question 1.1:** Define Software Engineering. State how it differs from ad-hoc computer programming.  
   *(Answer: IEEE definition: The systematic approach to the development, operation, maintenance, and retirement of software. Programming is writing code; SE encompasses full lifecycle management, quality assurance, cost control, and engineering rigor).*
2. **Question 1.2:** What is meant by the "Software Crisis"? State two historical causes.  
   *(Answer: The historical period where software projects routinely ran significantly over budget, missed delivery schedules, suffered from catastrophic bugs, and proved unmaintainable due to lack of systematic engineering practices).*
3. **Question 1.3:** State any two software myths believed by practitioners and their corresponding realities.  
   *(Answer: Myth 1: "Our job is done when we write working code." Reality: 60–80% of effort occurs post-deployment in maintenance. Myth 2: "Adding more programmers to a late project catches it up." Reality: Brooks' Law — Adding manpower to a late software project makes it later).*
4. **Question 1.4:** Why is the Classic Waterfall model considered a "linear sequential" process? State its primary limitation.  
   *(Answer: Each phase must finish before the next begins. Limitation: Working software is delivered very late in the lifecycle; highly inflexible to requirement changes).*
5. **Question 1.5:** Explain the core dual-pathway principle of the V-Model.  
   *(Answer: The left wing represents Verification (specification/design decomposition), and the right wing represents Validation (testing). Crucially, test plans for every level are created during the corresponding design phase).*
6. **Question 1.6:** Under what project conditions should the Rapid Application Development (RAD) model be chosen?  
   *(Answer: When well-understood, modular requirements exist, and delivery is urgently needed within a tight 60–90 day timeline using visual component-based 4GL tools).*
7. **Question 1.7:** Identify the 4 quadrants of Boehm's Spiral Model. What makes it unique among SDLC models?  
   *(Answer: (1) Determine objectives/alternatives, (2) Risk analysis and resolution, (3) Engineering and development, (4) Planning next phase. Unique feature: Explicit, continuous risk assessment at every iteration).*
8. **Question 1.8:** Distinguish between a Throwaway Prototype and an Evolutionary Prototype.  
   *(Answer: Throwaway prototypes are built quickly to clarify requirements and then discarded; Evolutionary prototypes begin with understood core features and evolve directly into the production system).*
9. **Question 1.9:** State the 4 foundational values of the Agile Manifesto.  
   *(Answer: (1) Individuals and interactions over processes and tools, (2) Working software over comprehensive documentation, (3) Customer collaboration over contract negotiation, (4) Responding to change over following a plan).*
10. **Question 1.10:** Define Extreme Programming (XP). What are the two distinct roles in Pair Programming?  
    *(Answer: An agile framework based on frequent releases in short development cycles. In Pair Programming, the **Driver** writes the code, while the **Navigator** reviews each line in real-time, considers strategic direction, and catches syntax/logical flaws).*
