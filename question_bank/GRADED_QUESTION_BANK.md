# 24ITPC302: Graded Question Bank (Multi-Tier Exam Collection)

**Course Code:** 24ITPC302 – Software Engineering  
**Regulations:** 2024 Autonomous (Anna University)

This graded question bank classifies examination questions across 5 difficulty and pedagogical tiers:

---

## 📑 Question Tier Index

1. **[Tier 1: Basic & Foundational Questions (K1 Level)](basic/BASIC_QUESTIONS.md):** 2-Mark short definitions, laws, acronyms, and direct recall.
2. **[Tier 2: Standard University Questions (K2/K3 Level)](standard/STANDARD_UNIVERSITY_QUESTIONS.md):** Textbook descriptive questions (7-Mark & 13-Mark standard models).
3. **[Tier 3: Confusing & Tricky Questions](confusing/TRICKY_EXAM_QUESTIONS.md):** Subtleties students frequently mix up (e.g. Verification vs Validation, Reverse Engineering vs Forward Engineering, Alpha vs Beta, Cohesion vs Coupling, Organic vs Embedded).
4. **[Tier 4: University Combo Questions (Full 13M / 14M Models)](university/UNIVERSITY_COMBO_MODELS.md):** Complete exam-tested combos across all 6 modules.
5. **[Tier 5: Advanced & Application Case Studies (Part C 15M)](advanced/ADVANCED_CASE_STUDIES.md):** Comprehensive real-world design, sizing, and architecture case studies (e.g. ATM, Hospital Management, Automated Train Control).

---
