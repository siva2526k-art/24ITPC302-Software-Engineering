## 🎯 4 Golden Rules for Tomorrow's Study Sessions

1. **Active Pen-and-Paper Practice for Diagrams:**  
   Don't just read diagrams on screen! Draw each of the **5 key diagrams** at least once on paper:
   - Barry Boehm's Spiral Model (4 Quadrants)
   - Pressman's 4-Level Software Design Pyramid
   - Petri Net Producer-Consumer ($N=1$ buffer)
   - CASE Environment Architecture (with the OMS Repository)
   - The V-Model (Verification left wing vs Validation right wing)
2. **Memorize the 3 COCOMO Equation Pairs:**  
   $$E = a \cdot (KLOC)^b \text{ PM}, \qquad D = c \cdot (E)^d \text{ Months}, \qquad P = \frac{E}{D} \text{ Persons}$$
   - Organic: $a=2.4, b=1.05, c=2.5, d=0.38$
   - Semidetached: $a=3.0, b=1.12, c=2.5, d=0.35$
   - Embedded: $a=3.6, b=1.20, c=2.5, d=0.32$
3. **Know the EVA Formulas by Heart:**  
   $$CV = EV - AC, \quad SV = EV - PV, \quad CPI = \frac{EV}{AC}, \quad SPI = \frac{EV}{PV}, \quad EAC = \frac{BAC}{CPI}$$
4. **Cohesion & Coupling Hierarchies:**  
   - Cohesion (Worst $\to$ Best): *Coincidental $\to$ Logical $\to$ Temporal $\to$ Procedural $\to$ Communicational $\to$ Sequential $\to$* **Functional (Best)**.
   - Coupling (Worst $\to$ Best): *Content (Worst) $\to$ Common $\to$ Control $\to$ Stamp $\to$* **Data (Best)** $\to$ *Uncoupled*.
